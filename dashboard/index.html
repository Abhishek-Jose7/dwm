"How will this analysis help the organization make better decisions and create business value?"

03

Monetizes APAC Volume

Resolves the regional disparity where APAC drives 29.5% of hours but only 13.8% of revenue through localized UPI and micro-pass auto-mandates.

Production DAX Measures for Power BI

Pre-tested formulas ready for direct insertion into Power BI reports
1. Total Gross Margin (INR) Total Gross Margin = SUM(Fact_Music_Operations[Monthly_Total_Revenue_INR]) - SUM(Fact_Music_Operations[Monthly_Licensing_Cost_INR])
2. Cohort Churn Rate (%) Cohort Churn Rate % = DIVIDE(CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(Fact_Music_Operations), Fact_Music_Operations[Churn_Binary] = 1), COUNTROWS(Fact_Music_Operations), 0)
3. Net Promoter Score (NPS) NPS Score = VAR P = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(Fact_Music_Operations), Fact_Music_Operations[NPS_Category] = "Promoter") VAR D = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(Fact_Music_Operations), Fact_Music_Operations[NPS_Category] = "Detractor") RETURN ROUND(DIVIDE(P - D, COUNTROWS(Fact_Music_Operations), 0) * 100, 0)
4. Ad Fatigue Ratio Ad Fatigue Ratio = DIVIDE(SUM(Fact_Music_Operations[Weekly_Ad_Impressions]), SUM(Fact_Music_Operations[Weekly_Listening_Hours]), 0)
